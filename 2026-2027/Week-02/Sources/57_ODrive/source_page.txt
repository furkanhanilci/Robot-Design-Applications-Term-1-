Important Note
The firmware in this repository is compatible with the ODrive v3.x (NRND) and is no longer under active development.
Firmware for the new generation of ODrives (
ODrive Pro
,
S1
,
Micro
, etc.) is currently being actively maintained and developed, however its source code is currently not publicly available. Access may be available under NDA, please
reach out to us
for inquiries.
Overview
