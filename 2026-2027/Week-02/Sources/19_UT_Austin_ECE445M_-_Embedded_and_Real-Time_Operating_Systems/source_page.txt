Recovering a dead MSPM0G3507
How to unbrick the MSPM0G3507:
Press-and-hold the BSL_Invoke button (S1, near LED) while pressing and releasing the Reset button.
The device should go to BSL and stay in Active mode for ~10secs.
Attempt to program immediately after releasing the reset button.
Release the BSL_Invoke button (S1, near LED).
Try performing a factory reset:
https://dev.ti.com/gallery/view/TIMSPGC/MSPM0_Factory_Reset_Tool/ver/1.0.2/
